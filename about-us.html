Who We Are

Built on Credentials,
Driven by Purpose

Turjumaan was founded in 2024 with a clear purpose: to make high-quality Arabic translation and Saudi university consultation genuinely accessible to Indian students and professionals. Too many applications fail not due to weak candidates, but due to translation errors, incomplete documentation, and unfamiliarity with institutional requirements.

Our founding team of six brings direct academic experience from Saudi Arabia's most respected institutions — the Islamic University of Madinah, King Khalid University in Abha, and Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University. All core translation work is executed or personally verified by these credentialed experts. No outsourcing. No shortcuts.
