Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

⏱️ How long does translation take? ▼

Standard translations typically take 5-7 days. Premium service delivers in 2-3 days, while Express service can be completed same-day depending on document length and complexity.

✅ Are translations notarized or certified? ▼

Yes! We provide certified translations with translator affidavit. For legal purposes, we can arrange notarization through authorized notaries in KSA and India.

📋 What documents do you translate? ▼

We translate academic documents (certificates, transcripts, degrees), professional documents (CVs, experience certificates), legal documents (contracts, agreements), and personal documents (passports, visas, birth certificates).

💰 How is pricing calculated? ▼

We charge per word of source text. Pricing depends on the language pair, document type, and urgency. Get a free quote by sending your document - no obligation!

🔐 Is my information confidential? ▼

Absolutely! We maintain strict confidentiality agreements and follow international privacy standards. Your documents are secure and handled by professional, verified translators only.
